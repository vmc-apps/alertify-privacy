Alertify - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Alertify ("the app") is designed with your privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Alertify.

No servers of our own

Alertify does not operate any servers. All incident data - photos, locations, and timestamps - is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive, see, or store this data ourselves.

Google user data

What Google user data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, Alertify requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send ("Send email on your behalf"). It also receives the email address of the Google account you sign in with, to show which account is connected. Alertify does not access your emails, contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data.

How the app uses Google user data

Alertify uses the gmail.send permission for one purpose only: to send you an alert email from your own Google account when (1) a wrong unlock code is entered on your device, or (2) the correct code is entered right after a wrong one. Each alert contains the photos, location and time of the incident and is sent only to the alert email address you chose in Settings. Alertify cannot read, list, search, modify or delete any email in your account.

Who the app shares Google user data with

Alertify does not share, transfer, sell or disclose Google user data to anyone. The alert emails are sent directly from your device to Google's Gmail service using your own account; no server operated by us is involved, and no third party receives your data.

How the app protects Google user data

The Google sign-in is handled by Google's official sign-in library, and Alertify never sees or stores your Google password. Alertify's access to your account is limited to sending email. All communication with Google's services is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Incident data (photos, locations, timestamps) is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access. Because Alertify has no servers, no Google user data is stored anywhere outside your device.

Data retention and deletion

Alertify does not retain Google user data on any server. On your device, incidents older than 90 days, or beyond the most recent 100 incidents, are automatically deleted together with their photos. You can revoke Alertify's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or disconnect the account inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Limited Use

Alertify's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing alert feature described above, is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop or train AI or machine learning models.

Camera

Alertify uses your device's front and back cameras solely to capture two photos at the moment a wrong unlock code is detected. The cameras are never used for continuous recording or monitoring.

Location

Alertify uses your device's location solely to record where an incident occurred, at the moment it happens. Location is never tracked continuously.

Email (Google Account)

Alertify sends incident alerts using your own connected Google account via the official Gmail API, with the gmail.send permission scope only. This means Alertify can send emails on your behalf but cannot read, view, or access any of your existing emails. We never see the content of the emails sent, and no email data passes through any server operated by us.

Device Administrator role

Alertify requests the Device Administrator role solely to detect when an incorrect unlock code is entered, even while the app is not open. This role cannot lock your device, erase data, or perform any action beyond this detection.

Motion Guard

If you enable the optional Motion Guard feature, Alertify uses your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) to detect movement while the feature is active. This data is processed only on your device, in real time, and is never stored or transmitted anywhere. If movement is detected, Alertify may sound a loud alarm to deter theft. The alarm stops automatically when you unlock your device normally.

Data retention

Incidents older than 90 days, or beyond the most recent 100 incidents, are automatically deleted from your device, including their associated photos.

No ads, no analytics, no third parties

Alertify does not display advertising, does not include analytics or tracking libraries, and does not share any data with third parties.

Purchases

Alertify offers a free 3-day trial and a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; Alertify never sees or stores your payment details.

Your control

You can change your alert email address, disconnect your Google account, or uninstall the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data.
